EmberSync
MODIS + VIIRS → one consistent burning-activity record

A single, sensor-agnostic fire calendar for any area on Earth

EmberSync merges every NASA FIRMS active-fire product (MODIS Terra/Aqua, VIIRS S-NPP / NOAA-20 / NOAA-21) into one de-duplicated, calibrated cell-day record, so a fire season from 2005 can be compared with one from 2025, and surges are flagged early. Demo dataset: Australia's Black Summer, — raw detections from 4 satellites.

Why harmonize? MODIS misses small fires

Of every cell-day VIIRS sees burning, the share MODIS also detects, by fire size (VIIRS FRP per cell-day). This bias is what breaks a naive MODIS→VIIRS time series.

Back-cast: VIIRS-equivalent activity from MODIS alone

Model trained Sep–Nov 2019, tested out-of-time Dec–Mar. This is how EmberSync extends a consistent record back to 2000 (pre-VIIRS).

How EmberSync works

A sensor-agnostic pipeline: a new satellite (e.g. NOAA-21, or future JPSS-3/4) is one adapter line, not a new product.

01Ingest & normalizeEvery FIRMS CSV variant → one schema. MODIS 0–100 confidence → l/n/h (FIRMS rule); footprint = scan×track.
02Local solar dayUTC + lon/15 h, so a 01:00 night overpass belongs to the same fire-day as the afternoon one.
03Static-source maskGas flares, industry, volcanoes, offshore learned from archive type + persistence, then applied to NRT data that lacks type.
04De-duplicateSame hotspot seen by 2–4 satellites the same day counts once (0.01° micro-grid).
05Fuse & calibrateCell-day fusion, noisy-OR confidence, FRP cross-calibration MODIS→VIIRS scale, sensitivity curve, back-cast model.
06Calendar & alertsAny bbox → daily calendar, new ignitions, escalating cells, surge alert. Exports CSV / Parquet / JSON API.
Data: NASA FIRMS — MODIS Collection 6 (MCD14ML, Terra & Aqua) and VIIRS 375 m (VNP14IMG S-NPP archive, VJ114IMG NOAA-20 NRT), LANCE/FIRMS, NASA EOSDIS. Basemap: Natural Earth. EmberSync is a NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 project — not an official NASA product. Region boxes are approximate bounding boxes.
